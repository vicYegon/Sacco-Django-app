# Problem Statement

## Who has the problem
Small and medium SACCOs in Kenya that manage members, savings and loans
using paper registers, spreadsheets and M-Pesa statements.

## What goes wrong today
- Member records are spread across books and spreadsheets, so details are
  duplicated, outdated or lost.
- Contributions are recorded by hand, so balances are slow to calculate
  and errors are hard to trace.
- Loan applications and guarantor approvals happen on paper, so members
  wait days for a decision and officers cannot see total guarantee exposure.
- Members cannot see their own balances or loan status without visiting
  the office.
- There is no reliable audit trail of who changed what.

## Impact
- Officers spend lots of time reconciling records.
- Members lose trust when balances are disputed.
- Loan risk is higher because eligibility and guarantees are checked manually.

## Goal for the MVP
A secure web platform where officers register members and record
contributions, members see their balances and apply for loans, and loan
officers review applications with eligibility and guarantee checks done
automatically, with every action recorded in an audit trail.

## How we will know it works (pilot success measures)
- All pilot members registered with no duplicate National IDs.
- Contribution balances match the SACCO's books at month-end reconciliation.
- A loan application can go from submission to decision within 1 or 2
  working days.
- No unauthorised access found in permission testing.
