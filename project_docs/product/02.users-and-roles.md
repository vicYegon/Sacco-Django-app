# Users and Roles

| Role | Who they are | Main goals |
|---|---|---|
| Member | A registered SACCO member | See balances and statements, apply for loans, respond to guarantor requests |
| SACCO officer | Front-office staff | Register members, update member details, record contributions |
| Loan officer | Credit staff | Review loan applications, check eligibility and guarantees, recommend or decide |
| Accountant | Finance staff | Approve disbursements, post repayments, reconcile balances, run reports |
| Administrator | System owner | Manage staff accounts, roles and system settings. Does not handle money. |
| Auditor | Internal or external reviewer | Read everything, change nothing |

## Rules
- A person can have more than one role only if it does not break
  separation of duties (see 03-permissions.md).
- Staff accounts are created by an Administrator. Members do not
  self-register in the MVP.
- Every staff action is recorded in the audit log.
